Privacy Policy for G Sports Center (GSC) Mobile App

Effective date: 2026-05-16

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data the GSC mobile app ("App") collects, how we use it, with whom we share it, how we store and protect it, and the rights you have regarding your data.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account & authentication: Firebase Authentication user ID (firebase_uid), email address, display name (if provided).
  • Profile & usage: optional profile details and usage data for analytics.
  • Purchases & finance: package purchases stored in Firestore and mirrored to `financial_transactions` in the website MySQL.
  • Payment proof: uploaded images stored in Firebase Storage or Cloudinary.
  • Device & diagnostics: device info, app version, crash reports.
  • Optional sensors/permissions: location, camera, media library, push token (only if you grant permission).

3. Why We Collect Data

Authentication, process purchases, validate payments, improve the App, send transactional messages, and comply with legal obligations.
